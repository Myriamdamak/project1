Score  Meaning
0      Incorrect or irrelevant
1      Partially correct, but has major errors
2      Mostly correct, with minor errors or omissions
3      Correct and sufficiently complete

Notes:
- "Major error": misstates the core purpose, inputs/outputs, or main control flow.
- "Minor error/omission": misses an edge case, side effect, or small detail but the overall purpose is right.
- Judge correctness against the code only. Length, tone, and formatting must not affect the score.
